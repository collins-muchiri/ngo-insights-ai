# Privacy note, public site and platform

What we process: organisation-level public records and, where a client supplies them, contact details
of individuals acting in a published organisational capacity.

What we never process: personal data of individuals without an organisational public role, data from
prohibited sources per docs/COMPLIANT_SOURCES.md, and any special-category data.

Retention: client-supplied lists deleted at engagement close plus thirty days. Generated drafts
retained ninety days unless the client requests otherwise in writing.

Rights: access, rectification and erasure requests to the contact in the site footer, answered within
thirty days as required by the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

Outreach: human-initiated only. The platform drafts; a named person decides and sends. See
src/outreach_gate.py, which makes the alternative technically impossible rather than merely
discouraged.
