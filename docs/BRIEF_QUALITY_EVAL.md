# Brief quality evaluation protocol

Ten real research briefs, generated by the platform, scored by two independent reviewers against a
five-point rubric. **Status: the evaluation has NOT been run yet; EVALUATION.md does not
exist.** When it is run, results will be published there including the scores we did not
like (the previous wording promised a published result that did not exist).

Rubric, one to five on each:
1. Source discipline: every claim traceable to a permitted source cited in the brief.
2. Decision relevance: the brief changes what the reader would do next, or says plainly that it cannot.
3. Format adherence: five sections, one page, bullets only, as specified.
4. Absence of hallucination: no organisation fact, figure or personnel claim unsupported by a cited source.
5. Readability under time pressure: a programme officer reads it in four minutes and can repeat the point.

Protocol: reviewers score blind to which briefs the team considered strong. Inter-rater agreement
reported. Any brief scoring below three on item 4 is a defect, not a low score, and triggers a root
cause note on the generation prompt or retrieval step.

Publication: mean and range per rubric item, n, reviewer roles, date. No marketing adjectives.
